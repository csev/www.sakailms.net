#SakaiCon2020 gets kudos for accessibility

Dec 16, 2020

1 min read

Sakai's annual online conference is among the best at providing accessibility for low-vision participants and presenters. #SakaiCon2020 took place in mid-November 2020 and featured a variety of faculty-friendly, instruction-centered presentations.

Accessibility strategy consultant Chris Knapp (the thought leader behind the Accessiversity blog) shares his thoughts about how conference organizers successfully help low-vision speakers get prepared and about his overall conference experience as a low-vision participant.
